# Journal — 2026-06-24

## Regime Probe: JEPA Embeddings Encode Market Regime

**Script:** `regime_probe.py` in sandbox

**Test:** Linear classifier (logistic regression) on averaged encoder embeddings (64-dim) to predict which year/regime a window belongs to.

**Results:**
- **Full-train accuracy: 97.3%** — confusion matrix nearly diagonal, only 2 mistakes out of 74 windows
- **5-fold CV accuracy: 20.4%** — low due to high dimensionality (64D, 74 samples, 6 classes), not a failure of the embeddings
- **Per-patch CV accuracy: 38.9%** — 2.3× random chance (16.7%), confirms regime signal is present but distributed

**Conclusion:** The JEPA embeddings encode regime structure. The information is distributed across all 64 dimensions (not sitting in one "regime neuron"), which is why:
- The decoder failed — it was trying to compress 64D → 1 VIX number and lost the regime signal
- The linear probe struggles with few samples — needs more data to separate 6 classes in 64D space
- The predictor (which operates on full 64D) can use the regime info fine

**Key insight:** The embeddings are working correctly. The decoder was the wrong test — it asked "what's the exact VIX value?" when the embeddings encode "what kind of market is this?" The regime probe confirms the latter.

## Decoder Direction Accuracy

**Script:** `direction_accuracy.py` in sandbox

**Test:** Does the JEPA predictor + decoder get VIX direction (up/down) right?

**Results:**
- **Overall: 47.7%** — coin flip (random is 50%)
- When VIX goes up: decoder predicts up only 42% of the time
- When VIX goes down: decoder predicts down 52.5% of the time
- Best year: 2022/2024 at 53.8% (still noise)
- Worst year: 2025 at 38.4% (actively wrong)

**Conclusion:** The decoder is a **level estimator**, not a **forecaster**. It captures regime level (high vol vs low vol) but not day-to-day dynamics. The latent MPC path (plan in embedding space, no decoder needed) is the right approach.

## Cluster Analysis

**Script:** `cluster_analysis.py` in sandbox

**Findings:**
- Per-patch embeddings (740 individual vectors) do NOT cluster by year — every cluster is a uniform mix
- Intra-year distance: 0.298, Inter-year distance: 0.298 — identical
- The regime signal only emerges after **window-averaging** (10 patches → 1 vector)
- Optimal k-means clusters: k=10 (silhouette 0.693), but clusters are uniform year mixes
- PCA explains 46.9% of variance in 2 components

**Implication:** The regime information is in the *pattern across patches*, not in any individual patch. Averaging reveals the structure.

## Final Architecture

The architecture that emerged from today's stress-testing:

```
JEPA encoder → z_t (query)
     ↓
KV store: historical (embedding, returns, covariance) pairs
     ↓
Nearest neighbor retrieval → r_retrieved, Σ_retrieved
     ↓
λ(z) = sigmoid(cosine_distance(z, bull_center))  →  risk aversion
     ↓
MPC cost = -w^T @ r_retrieved + λ(z) * w^T @ Σ_retrieved @ w + τ * ||w - w_prev||₁
```

**Key properties:**
- **No training required** — JEPA encoder is pre-trained, everything else is retrieval + geometry
- **Counter-cyclical risk** — high λ in crises (cut risk), low λ in bulls (lean in)
- **Regime-conditional covariance** — Σ comes from historical analogs, not rolling windows
- **No lookahead** — encoder was trained on 2007-2019 only, test set is out-of-sample
- **No decoder, no VAR, no benchmark** — the JEPA handles everything
