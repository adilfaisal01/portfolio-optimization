## 2026-06-08 — JEPA Integration
**Last commit:** `8f1efbc` — "feat: optimize predictor argument order for clarity"

- Docker infrastructure: Dockerfile, docker-compose.yaml, entrypoint.sh

**Decisions:**
- **JEPA architecture adopted**: Integrated TS-JEPA from Sennadir et al. (2025) — self-supervised learning for time series
- **Encoder**: Conv1D tokenizer → Transformer (4 layers, 8 heads, embed_dim=256) → LayerNorm
- **Predictor**: Linear projection → Transformer (2 layers, 4 heads, predictor_embed_dim=512) → output projection
- **Decoder**: Simple linear layer (emb_dim → patch_size) — placeholder for downstream tasks
- **Data pipeline**: `StockMarketJEPADataset` concatenates ETF features (log_return, hl_spread, RVOL) + macro features + VIX into 49-dim vector, splits into 20-patch windows with random masking
- **Training loop**: EMA encoder (momentum=0.998), L1 prediction loss, AdamW optimizer
- **Docker infrastructure**: Dockerfile + docker-compose.yaml + entrypoint.sh for GPU training on RunPod

**Working:** JEPA training loop ran end-to-end. Encoder and predictor architectures correct. Data loading from parquet worked.
**Not working:** **Representation collapse** — mean pairwise cosine similarity of 0.9997, all embeddings nearly identical. Linear probe accuracy (57.0%) barely above majority baseline (54.5%). Only 6 effective dimensions out of 256 for 90% variance. No VICReg or regularization to prevent collapse.
